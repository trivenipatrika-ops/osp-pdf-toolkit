Privacy Policy

OSP PDF Toolkit · Last updated: September 2026

Summary: OSP PDF Toolkit does not upload, collect, or store your files, photos, or documents on any server. All PDF, image resize, and compression processing happens entirely on your own device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not read or access the content of your files.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

OSP PDF Toolkit is designed to work fully offline for its core features. When you use this app to convert images to PDF, merge or split PDFs, resize images, or compress files, that processing happens locally on your device. We never see, upload, transmit, or store the content of any file you work with in this app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access to the app. Google AdMob may collect certain data to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance, which can include:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. You can control ad personalization through your device's Google Settings ("Ads" section) at any time.

3. Permissions We Use

  • Internet access — required to load ads and app assets.
  • File storage access — used only to let you save or share the files you create within the app (such as a compressed image or merged PDF). We do not scan, browse, or access any other files on your device.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising served through this app follows Google's applicable child-safety advertising policies.

5. Data Security

Since your files are processed entirely on your device and never transmitted to us, there is no server-side storage of your personal documents or images to secure or lose. Standard Android app sandboxing protects your data from other apps.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
